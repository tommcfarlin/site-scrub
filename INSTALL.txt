Site Scrub 🧽 - how to install

1. Keep the unzipped folder somewhere permanent. The browser reads the extension from this folder every time it starts.
2. Open brave://extensions. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
3. Turn on Developer mode. This switch is at the top right.
4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
5. Click the puzzle-piece menu and pin the 🧽.
6. Right-click the 🧽, choose Options, and pick the sites to scrub.

Use it

Click the 🧽. Press Scrub in the box. Scrubbing is permanent.

Update

Download the new zip. Unzip it over the old folder. Click the reload button on Site Scrub in brave://extensions.

The full guide is at https://github.com/tommcfarlin/site-scrub
